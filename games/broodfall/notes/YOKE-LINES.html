YOKE's Lines

One campaign, step by step, in the order a player meets her: each step is an event, what happens is said under it, then every line she can say there. Click a line to edit it in place, mark a line Cut, or leave a note. Export changes saves your edits to this page's own store, where the next session picks them up: nothing to paste. Once a change is in the files, it clears from here on your next visit; what you have not exported stays. An italic line under a greeting is what she knows behind it: her live mind gets it, the player never sees it.
