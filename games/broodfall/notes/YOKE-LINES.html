YOKE's Lines

Connecting…

Every line of hers, in six parts. 1. The story lines, each once a campaign, in the order they play. 2. Her everyday lines after a mission. 3. The triggered lines, by rank: on any return the highest-ranked line that is due plays, and the rest wait for the next return. 4. What she says outside the talk after a mission, 5. her live mind's pools, and 6. the films. Each step starts with TRIGGER: what makes it play. Click a line to edit it in place, switch Keep to Remove to cut it, leave a note, or tick it Reviewed. The review is shared: everyone who opens it (any browser, any Claude account) sees the same edits, notes and ticks, kept in one shared store that every session reads (on claude.ai, the page's own store; on the PC, notes/yoke-review.json). Export changes hands them to the next session: nothing to paste. Once a change is in the files it clears from here for everyone. A tick holds until the line is rewritten. New marks a line whose words changed since you last opened the page. An italic line under a greeting is what she knows behind it: her live mind gets it, the player never sees it. The films come last: every cut scene with its film to play and every line of it, edited the same way (a changed line means that film is re-shot).

This is the cloud copy, for any computer (Oct 18 2026). Its review is kept in this page's own store on claude.ai, which every Claude session reads. It is separate from the PC copy (http://localhost:8792/, notes/yoke-review.json).
