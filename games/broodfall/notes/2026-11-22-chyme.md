# Chyme: the organ stage's own currency (Nov 22 2026)

Collins: "the organ stage is using the same currency as the limb stage this is not good game design a secondary currency
like processed food should build for the organ stage based on what you earned not what you had left (and make sure you
think through the economics of this)". Design, numbers and decisions: DESIGN.md "CHYME". Engineering: HANDOFF.md "Chyme".

## Iterations (played in the page, seed 7, then measured)
1. **15% of the meat digested + a ration of 36 + 3/wave.** Modelled and measured fine (1,177 a run, all organs, 5 levels)
   with the war wage halved (139 limbs, 10/10: too easy) and then with no war wage (119 limbs, 9/10: the limbs' budget as
   before). Played: wave 1 rendered 0 from its bodies ("0 from the meat it digested · 39 ration"), wave 3 rendered 3. The
   link between eating and organs was invisible in exactly the waves that teach it.
2. **Per body: war 1, science 2, court 4, + 30 + 1/wave.** Played: wave 1 rendered 3 from its bodies (only 3 of 8 had been
   digested at the clear) and 34 in all: one short of the Gut, so the first organ stage bought nothing. Empty first stage.
3. **Per body + a flat 38 a clear (built).** Played: 41, 52, 43, 78 chyme after waves 1-4; the Gut, the Bone Forge, a
   bladder and pacemaker, a heart, a lattice and a meteor level bought by clicks; the HUD counter climbs with every body
   ("+10 this wave"); the feed names "+1 ◆ chyme" per body. Measured 20 seeds: 17/20 (18/20 before), 122 limbs (121), 22.4
   organs (21.6), 6.3 levels (1.8).

## Personas
- **Newcomer** (mission 1, fresh profile): the ◆ CHYME box is hidden at the start and glows in at the first digested body,
  while the coach says "every body the creep digests pays WAR meat for cards, and ◆ CHYME for organs". The organ stage after
  wave 1: "WAVE 1 EARNED +45 · 7 from the bodies it digested · 38 for the clear", chyme 45, and a heart, a root, a bladder or
  a level of the Gut / Forge / Meteor affordable. One confusion left: the box's label shows a small dot before the ◆ (the
  meat boxes' caste dot); harmless.
- **Genre veteran** (Ball x Pit's town currency, Slay the Spire's gold vs. card rewards, Kingdom Rush's stars): a second
  currency for the between-runs layer, earned by play, never by what the battle left over; separate wallets, so each layer
  is its own decision. YES on: earned not leftover, visible as it accrues, the tally at the end of the wave, prices on cards
  in the new unit, the old currency untouched for its own layer.
- **Breaker:** (a) hoard war to farm chyme: renders nothing (tested). (b) Ignore organs and pour everything into limbs:
  chyme cannot buy limbs; the clearing wage that used to be war is now chyme, so ignoring organs throws it away. (c) Ignore
  limbs: meat cannot buy organs. (d) Stall a wave to farm: the bodies are a fixed list; parties come in the first 36 s only;
  the wave is called off 60 s after its last entry. (e) Bank chyme for a late burst: allowed, and worse than buying early
  (an organ works every wave after it grows). No degenerate line found.
