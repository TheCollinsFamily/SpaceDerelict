# The first-boot film: "THE THING FROM THE SKY" (Oct 1 2026)

Collins (Oct 1 2026): "create an intro video that plays the first time a user boots up the software ... some
sort of 1950 B movie style scene from the game from the perspective of people in whatever the starting biome
of something coming from the sky and have that replace the intro vid for the very first mission (not revealing
the ship and everything until that mission's over)".

- **Where:** the Crash Site, mission 1's territory, drawn in the Suburbs tile set: Luckwell's company suburb
  of pastel paper houses (mint, butter yellow, powder blue, salmon pink), wax picket fences, round windows,
  striped awnings, dandelion-clock trees, beetle-shaped cars, a civil-defence siren on the corner, TV aerials.
  The meteor falls "between a school and a laundromat" (content/lore/insects.md, Night 0).
- **When:** night (canon: "It was a quiet night, in a quiet little town"). The game's board is day; the film
  ends on the crater, and mission 1 starts at the crash site the morning after (no landing film after it).
- **Who:** only the insect people (workers are female: most townsfolk are women). Never a human, a ship, the
  Empire, YOKE or the player. Nobody learns what fell.
- **Frame:** 4:3 (the stills keep everything in the middle; the 16:9 clips are cut to 4:3 at the bake),
  faded 1950s Technicolor (the probe below), grain, gate weave, dust and scratches, a soft vignette, a flicker.
- **After the impact, nothing tells the audience what it can see** (Collins, Oct 1 2026: "it just sort of undermines
  the magnitude of what's happening ... no shit there is a giant thing taking over the town"). The narrator stops at the
  sky (n1-n3); no title card after the impact but the closing one; the second half is carried by the pictures and the
  sound alone. Spoken words are never written in capitals: a video model reads an all-caps word as an acronym and
  spells it ("nothing E V E R happens"); emphasis goes in the prompt (`stress`), and `speakable` refuses such a line.
- **Sound:** one mix inside the film: the score (theremin, tremolo strings, brass stabs, timpani), the trailer
  narrator, the townsfolk's screams, the roar and the strike. Titles are set in TYPE by the game over the film
  (never lettering inside a picture), timed on the film's own clock.
