# The post-game: the Roach King's simulation (design, Oct 12 2026)

> **Oct 15 2026:** Collins gave the frame (the map resets, you keep your upgrades, everything is harder) and the beats of
> his last film (he digitises in a field, you talk to him). The lap is the frame now and this design is the twist inside
> a lap: `notes/ROACH-KING-ARCHIVE-2026-10-15.md`.

Collins (Oct 12 2026), on the to-do board's "Hide the side orders on the last mission": "that's a bad idea, we just
haven't built the post game yet (the roach king's simulation as he gets disappointed in the end when the war is over and
you're like well I could use practice) think through the mechanics on this."

So the Requisition Board stays on the last mission: its reward is spent here. Nothing below is built.

## The premise, from the lore we have

- Everyone the asset absorbs goes to **the archive**, a world cut to their desires (DESIGN.md "The reveal"; each finale
  says it). The Roach King's heaven, as proposed in `content/lore/roach-king.md`: "a stream with a billion viewers and
  every one of them agreeing."
- That is the joke, and the engine of the post-game: **he never wanted the viewers, he wanted the fight.** A billion
  antennae agreeing with him is the most boring thing that has ever happened to him. The war is over, he lost, and he is
  bored in paradise.
- So he does what a streamer does: **he builds a custom game.** He has the archive print him the planet again, every
  territory, and challenges the Crater Thing to a rematch, on his rules, live.
- **He still never meets the operator** (the rule of Oct 1 stands). He challenges the Thing. The player plays the Thing.
  YOKE renders him, as always; the ship's survey catches the stream from inside the asset.
- The Technician's side of it is Collins's line: "well, I could use practice." The Index office is waiting, the next
  posting is coming; the asset's own archive is the one range on which nobody dies.

## How it opens

1. The last mission is won (`ended`). YOKE's ending greeting; the desk says CAMPAIGN COMPLETE, as today.
2. **One more address**, caught where the others were, but the band reads differently: `THE HIVE HOUSE STREAM · FROM THE
   ARCHIVE · viewers 1,000,000,000 · all agreeing`. Him in his den again (the den still, printed by the archive, a little
   too perfect: no takeout boxes). Disappointed: "Chat. The war's over. We lost. I'm in heaven. Everybody agrees with me.
   It is the worst thing that has ever happened to me." Then the turn: "Crater Thing. I know you can hear me. You ate
   me. Rude. Rematch. My rules. Tonight." The gun. (One clip set, den still: about $2 to $3.)
3. The desk changes: the globe is **his print** (a palette shift, PRINTED BY THE ARCHIVE across the top), every territory
   of the campaign open at once, the Hive House too. A MATCH CARD sits where the briefing was.

## A match

One deployment on any territory, under his rules. The card is a seeded roll of three things he reads out:

| Part | What it is | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| The ground | any territory (all unlocked: it is a print) | `TERRITORIES` |
| The kind | hold / royal / harvest today; the NEW kinds (board #4) are tested here first, as his custom games, and promoted to campaign territories once they play well | `directive` + new kinds |
| One or two rules | "Chat voted: no fire tonight", "double Host", "the big girls arrive on turn one", "fog: no fliers", "everything respawns", "no eating" | `Partial<SimConfig>` (the experiments already do this: `trapCage`, `matingMusk`, `lateHost`) |

- **No two cards alike in a run of twenty** (seeded order per pass through the kinds and rules, as the faction asides do
  it: `asideIndex`).
- Win or lose, the campaign is not touched: nothing is captured, nothing lost. The ground stays his print.

## What it pays, and what notes are for (board #14)

- **Standing is not earned in the archive.** Command does not sanction practice matches; the standing orders close
  with the war. The licence and the sanctioned catalogue are campaign things.
- **Field notes are the archive's currency**: a won match pays notes (his dares, the same `DARES` table, two a card);
  the notes buy:
  - the unsanctioned lineages not yet bought (Marrow, Resonance);
  - **his rule cards**: re-roll the next card (2 notes) or pick its kind and rule (4);
  - **the zoo**: borrow a lineage never bought, for one match (1 note; "I printed all your organs. Chat has your
    blueprints.");
  - nothing else. Notes that pile up in the campaign with nothing to buy are the stake of the post-game: that is the
    answer to #14, and why the Requisition Board stays visible on the last mission (#7).

## His arc across the matches (cards first, clips when approved)

At most one line a match, in order, never repeated; his addresses' rules (`src/meta/roachKing.ts`) carry it.

| After match | Mood | The line (draft; his voice bible holds) |
|---|---|---|
| the opening | disappointed | the address above |
| each won by the Thing | sore loser | a pool of six: "Chat says I let you win. Chat is WRONG. Chat is banned." |
| each won by him | gracious, briefly | a pool of six: "Good game. That's a lie. Great game. For me." |
| 3 | the turn | "Tonight I'm playing. Me. Personally. I printed a beetle." (see #6 below) |
| 5 | lifting | "This is better than the real war. Nobody dies. Everybody respawns. It is GREAT." |
| 10 | at peace | "Chat, I think I'm happy. Don't tell anyone. This concludes the address!" The gun. |
| after 10 | the pool | his lines are the sore-loser and gracious pools; the stream never ends |

- **He never learns about the operator.** His lines address the Thing and chat. YOKE's notes stay under them.
- **The President on the field (board #6).** The one place he can stand on the board without breaking the rule is his
  own simulation. From match 3 a rule card reads THE PRESIDENT IS ON THE FIELD: a boss body (him on the gilded war
  beetle, the gold machine gun, the flag cape; one body, one behaviour: he follows the Host's biggest wave, fires in
  bursts, and the match is lost for him when the beetle falls). Phase 2: it needs its art.

## What YOKE says

A small pool for the archive (six after a won match, six after a lost one, in her register: "You're practising
genocide. On a photocopy. I'm not judging. I'm logging."), and one line each for the opening and for the tenth match.
Each said once; the news from Earth when the pool is dry (the rule of Oct 12 2026, `greetingFor`).

## What carries to the next campaign

Nothing but the comics (the rule). The archive earns comics (board #9): the comic of a match, drawn from its debrief
still, at milestones: the first match won, five won, the first match won under THE PRESIDENT IS ON THE FIELD. Three
comics, two pages each. That is the long-term hook: a player who has finished the campaign keeps coming back for the
shelf.

## State and screens

- `CampaignState.archive?: { matches: number; won: number; card: MatchCard; said: string[]; comics: string[] }`; `ended`
  stays as it is. A save that ended before this exists gets the archive on its next load (as `migrateFinale` did).
- The desk in archive mode: the globe's palette, every territory open, the match card instead of the briefing, DEPLOY as
  today. The debrief as today, plus his line and the notes paid. The Gene Bay as today (the zoo's borrowed lineage marked
  BORROWED). The Comms room lists his archive addresses with the others.
- Settings: none new.

## Phases and cost

1. **No new art** (content + rules): the match cards, the archive desk, the rules as `SimConfig` partials, his lines and
   YOKE's as CARDS, notes as the stake, the three comics. About the size of the last mission's build (Oct 4).

3. **The President on the field**: the boss body and its behaviour (board #6), and the new mission kinds promoted from
   his custom games to campaign territories (board #4).
