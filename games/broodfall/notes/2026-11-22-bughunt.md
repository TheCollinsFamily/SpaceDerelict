## 1. The board did not fill the window (FIXED)
- **Seen:** the board was a fixed 1360x1000 picture shrunk into the middle of the stage. On his 2781x1401 window it
  took 1360 of 2781 pixels across (black either side); at 1920x1080 it was 1161 wide; at 1366x768, 711.
- **Cause:** `IsoRenderer.init` / `Renderer.init` made a 1360x1000 canvas and the CSS only shrank it
  (`max-width/max-height: 100%`). `notes/GRAPHICS-PLAN.md` item 4 had planned "canvas fills the window"; never done.
- **Fix:** `src/main.ts` `fitBoardToStage`: the renderer is resized to the stage's size (ResizeObserver, so a window
  resize follows); `src/style.css` the canvas fills the stage. Both renderers frame the city from the renderer's own
  size, so the camera, clicks, zoom and turning need nothing else.
- **Proof:** canvas 2781x1175 at 2781x1401, 1920x854 at 1920x1080, 1366x523 at 1366x768; the city drawn larger on his
  window (scale 0.64 -> 0.76); limbs placed by real clicks at 2781; 60 fps in a wave; no console errors.

## 1b. The landing film's last frame on the board, with the canvas filling the window (FIXED, same day)
- **Risk the coordinator caught:** each landing film ends on a picture of the board as drawn on the old 1360x1000
  canvas at the default camera, laid on the live canvas by `filmBoxOnBoard` assuming that canvas. After item 1 the canvas
  is the window's size and the board is framed at another scale, so the picture landed far off (at 1920x1080 the old
  mapping drew the film 1920 px wide instead of 1164 and 81 px too high; at 2781x1401 about 940 px off).
- **Fix:** `IsoRenderer.fitAt(w, h)` tells the scale the same board would be framed at on the films' 1360x1000 canvas,
  `scaleNow()` the live one; `src/meta/landing.ts filmBoxOnBoard(..., view)` scales the picture by live/film and pins its
  crater on the live meteor; `landingBoard()` (main.ts) passes the view. Old canvas + old framing gives the old box.
- **Test:** `tests/landing.test.ts`: on the old canvas nothing changes; on a window-sized canvas every board point of the
  film lands on the same point of the live board (also with the canvas drawn at half its pixels).
- **Proof in the page:** the film's end picture laid at 50% over the live board at the handoff box lines up exactly at
  1366x768, 1920x1080, 2781x1401 (suburb) and 1920x1080 (megacity); the old box beside it for contrast.
