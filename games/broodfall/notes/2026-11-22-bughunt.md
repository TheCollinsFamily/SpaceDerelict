## 1. The board did not fill the window (FIXED)
- **Seen:** the board was a fixed 1360x1000 picture shrunk into the middle of the stage. On his 2781x1401 window it
  took 1360 of 2781 pixels across (black either side); at 1920x1080 it was 1161 wide; at 1366x768, 711.
- **Cause:** `IsoRenderer.init` / `Renderer.init` made a 1360x1000 canvas and the CSS only shrank it
  (`max-width/max-height: 100%`). `notes/GRAPHICS-PLAN.md` item 4 had planned "canvas fills the window"; never done.
- **Fix:** `src/main.ts` `fitBoardToStage`: the renderer is resized to the stage's size (ResizeObserver, so a window
  resize follows); `src/style.css` the canvas fills the stage. Both renderers frame the city from the renderer's own
  size, so the camera, clicks, zoom and turning need nothing else.
- **Proof:** canvas 2781x1175 at 2781x1401, 1920x854 at 1920x1080, 1366x523 at 1366x768; the city drawn larger on his
  window (scale 0.64 -> 0.76); limbs placed by real clicks at 2781; 60 fps in a wave; no console errors.
