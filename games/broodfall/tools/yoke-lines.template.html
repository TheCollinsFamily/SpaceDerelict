YOKE's Lines

Connecting…

One campaign, step by step, in the order a player meets her: each step is an event, what happens is said under it, then every line she can say there. Click a line to edit it in place, mark it Cut, leave a note, or tick it Reviewed. The review is shared: everyone who opens it (any browser, any Claude account) sees the same edits, notes and ticks, kept in one file on this PC that every session reads. Export changes hands them to the next session: nothing to paste. Once a change is in the files it clears from here for everyone. A tick holds until the line is rewritten. New marks a line whose words changed since you last opened the page. An italic line under a greeting is what she knows behind it: her live mind gets it, the player never sees it. The films come last: every cut scene with its film to play and every line of it, edited the same way (a changed line means that film is re-shot).
