# The Technopuritan Universe (shared lore)

Working notes — extend as games need it, keep games consistent with it.

## The Technopuritan Empire

A far-future authoritarian human empire. Expansionist ("living room" doctrine: habitable worlds are humanity's by right), bureaucratic, and doctrinally materialist — its theology cashes out in history and institutions, not an afterlife. Personal advancement is licensed: rank, resources, and the right to a mate are earned through service metrics.

The Empire's ethical horizon stops at the human species line. Xenofauna clearance — including sapient xenofauna — is filed under pest control: low-status, proceduralized, boring to command, and utterly unexamined.

## Known regions and factions

- **Techopuritan Crusade Zones** — active expansion fronts (already on Space Derelict's sector map as a high-risk region).
- **The rim** — lawless salvage space where Space Derelict's brutality game-show culture thrives outside imperial norms.
- **The eusocial worlds** — planets held by intelligent eusocial insectoid civilizations, target of the clearance campaigns in Broodfall.

## Tone rules

- The Empire's own media voice is chipper, propagandistic, 1950s-newsreel confident. In-world documents are written in that voice.
- The horror is never announced; it leaks through procedure (memo language, metrics, forms) and through cracks in the propaganda filter.
- The Empire is played straight. Nothing in universe docs winks at the camera.
